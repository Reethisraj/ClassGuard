CLASSGUARD CLASSROOM ACCESS POLICY

RULE 1 - USER ELIGIBILITY
Verified students and faculty members can submit classroom requests.

RULE 2 - PERMITTED PURPOSE
Classrooms can be requested for academic activities such as project work,
study sessions, seminars, presentations and approved club activities.

RULE 3 - LARGE GROUP REQUEST
Requests involving more than 20 students require faculty approval.

RULE 4 - ROOM CAPACITY
The selected classroom must have sufficient seating capacity for the
number of students in the request.

RULE 5 - TIMETABLE CONFLICT
A classroom cannot be approved if it is already scheduled for another
class or academic activity during the requested time.

RULE 6 - PROJECTOR REQUIREMENT
If a projector is requested, the selected classroom must have a projector.

RULE 7 - LABORATORY ACCESS
Laboratory rooms require authorization from the concerned department.

RULE 8 - OUTSIDE HOURS
Requests outside normal college working hours require administrative review.

RULE 9 - STUDENT AUTHORIZATION
Students can submit classroom requests, but requests requiring additional
approval must be forwarded to the appropriate faculty or administrator.

RULE 10 - EXPLAINABLE DECISION
Every classroom access decision should provide the reason for approval,
review or rejection.
